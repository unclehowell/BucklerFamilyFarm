How to read the counts

For answered items, the green figure is the calendar-day interval from the date asked to the date the reply was received. For unanswered items, the red figure is the calendar-day interval from the first date shown to 17 November 2026. Where a question was later narrowed or repeated, the information button records both dates. A refusal is shown as a response because a decision was communicated; it is not described as disclosure.
