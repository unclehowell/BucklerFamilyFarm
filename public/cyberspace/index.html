LOADED PROFILESHOOTER / SHIELD BREAKER

01 / MOVE

Arrow keys or W, A, S, D. A gamepad uses its D-pad or left stick. On phones, press and hold the on-screen directional pad.

02 / ACTION

SPACE or gamepad A; press and hold the ACTION button. It fires in BP Invaders, triggers a trace pulse in Title Chain, and gives a short dash in Archive Run ’88.

03 / SHIELD & PAUSE

X or SHIFT, gamepad B, or the SHIELD button gives a brief shield. Press P / ESC or gamepad START to pause. Touch buttons work by holding them down.
