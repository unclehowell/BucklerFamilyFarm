7. The Family's position on Parcel A and the estate

Our case, prospective. Not a finding of any court.

  1. 7.1The Family asserts unextinguished equitable ownership of the land known as Parcel A at Tŷ Mawr (Great House Farm), Llandough-juxta-Penarth, and of everything on and within it; and of the items the Family asserts were removed from the home without its consent, including furniture and artefacts now held by others or by institutions.
  2. 7.2The Family's position is that, although it lost possession and the legal title, its equitable ownership was never extinguished. It is seeking the restoration of its property and intends to bring its case before the High Court, in the King's Bench Division and the Chancery Division.
  3. 7.3The Family is also researching the wider Tŷ Mawr / Cedwyn estate in Leckwith and Llandough, and reserves its position in respect of that estate.
  4. 7.4The Family records that in BP Properties Ltd v Buckler [1987] EWCA Civ 2 the Court of Appeal decided against the Family on possession (judgment: Legal Wiki EV-0080). That decision stands as the court's ruling unless and until it is set aside or overtaken.
  5. 7.5This section is a statement of the Family's position and of the claim it intends to pursue. It does not state or imply that any named person, company or institution has acted unlawfully.
