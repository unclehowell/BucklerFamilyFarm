A reconstruction of Great House Farm, the Tŷ Mawr farmhouse of Llandough, is planned for Puerto Madryn, the town where Welsh settlers landed in 1865.

A poetic parallel

Two shores, one remembered home

From Cardiff and the sea to Tŷ Mawr; from Puerto Madryn and the Atlantic to a house that remembers Wales.

The search south of Puerto Madryn will test whether the relationship between the original house, town and coast can be echoed in a new landscape. This is a geographic hypothesis to test, not a measured fact yet. GIS work will compare georeferenced points, routes, topography, access, utilities and protected-area constraints.

The Welsh in Patagonia

On 28 July 1865 the ship Mimosa landed about 150 Welsh settlers at Puerto Madryn (Porth Madryn). They founded Y Wladfa, the Welsh settlement of the Chubut valley, and Welsh is still spoken there today.

A Welsh farmhouse rebuilt in Puerto Madryn would bring Tŷ Mawr to the town where that story began.

Delivery plan

From tribute to a buildable project

1 · Research and reconnaissance

Reconcile the acreage history; screen areas south of Puerto Madryn for title, zoning, habitat, climate risks, water, access and services.

2 · Concept and community

Prepare a measured Welsh house brief, climate-adapted options and early local cultural conversations.

3 · Business case and permissions

Obtain local quotations, planning and legal advice, operating costs, visitor-demand evidence and a funding strategy.

4 · Phased opening

Prioritise an accessible core museum and grounds. Add later amenities only when justified and funded.

No construction budget or opening date is published. Cost and schedule baselines follow site, feasibility and permissions work.

Philanthropy · partnership · grants

A founder-funded act of remembrance

The founding intention is for Sion Buckler to finance early work as philanthropy: UK legal research, the Welsh virtual reconstruction, documentary preservation, the online presence and initial Argentina feasibility studies. Expenditure will be recorded by workstream through the Project Office.
