The Welsh in Patagonia

On 28 July 1865 the ship Mimosa landed about 150 Welsh settlers at Puerto Madryn (Porth Madryn). They founded Y Wladfa, the Welsh settlement of the Chubut valley, and Welsh is still spoken there today.

A Welsh farmhouse rebuilt in Puerto Madryn would bring Tŷ Mawr to the town where that story began.
